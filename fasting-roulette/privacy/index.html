The short version

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Richard Athey, operating as Chipboard ("Chipboard," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information in the Fasting Roulette Android application (the "App") and on this privacy-policy page.

The App is a local-first fasting timer and survival game. It does not require registration and does not send your fasting history, game history, or settings to servers operated by us.

About this webpage

This page is static. It does not use analytics, cookies, advertising, forms, or browser storage. External services are contacted only if you choose to follow an external link.

What exists, and where

2. Information we handle

Information stored on your device

To provide the App's features, Fasting Roulette stores the following information locally on your Android device:

  • fast start, planned-end, and actual-end times; selected duration; and completion status;
  • refeed timing, fasting history, streaks, and aggregate progress statistics;
  • survival-game history, random outcomes, resources, and progress;
  • preferences such as theme, animation, haptics, timer duration, onboarding status, and notification choices; and
  • advertising consent choices stored by Google's consent tools.

Some of this information may reflect health-related habits. We do not receive it, upload it to our own servers, link it to your identity, or use it to build a profile about you.

Information you choose to send us

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, name, message, and any attachments. We use it to respond, provide support, investigate issues, and protect the App. Please do not email sensitive health information.

Information we do not request

The App does not ask for your name, postal address, email address, phone number, date of birth, weight, medical conditions, medications, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

The third-party service

3. Google advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent where required. At each launch, the App asks Google's consent service whether advertising is permitted and shows a consent message when required for your region. That service may process the device and consent information needed to determine and remember which privacy choices apply.

According to Google's Mobile Ads data-disclosure documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and ad interactions, including app launches and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data; and
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your consent choices, device settings, region, and eligibility, Google may serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Fasting Roulette does not give Google your locally stored fasting or game records.

Google and participating advertising partners handle information under their own policies. You can learn more from:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK data disclosure

Access the App may request

4. Device permissions

Notifications

Optional notification access lets the App alert you about fast completion, halfway and one-hour milestones, or hunt availability, according to your choices.

Alarms and restart

Exact-alarm access, when you grant it, improves alert timing. Restart access lets the App restore scheduled alerts after a reboot or app update.

Vibration

Vibration supports haptic feedback and notifications. You can turn haptics off in the App's settings.

Internet and network state

Network access is used for Google consent and advertising services. The core timer, history, and game operate with local data.

These permissions do not give the App access to the contents of your notifications, calendar, contacts, files, precise location, camera, or microphone.

Why information is used

5. How information is used and shared

Local information is used to run the timer, preserve progress, calculate statistics, restore active fasts after the App closes or the device restarts, remember settings, and schedule the notifications you choose.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your locally stored fasting history, game history, or settings with other companies. Google may process and share advertising-related information as described above. Under some privacy laws, personalized advertising may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" even when no money is exchanged.

We may disclose information you send to us if reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our rights, prevent fraud or harm, or respond to a valid legal request. Our email provider may process support messages on our behalf.

How long information remains

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local App data remains on your device until you reset the App's data, clear its storage in Android settings, or uninstall it.
  • Android backups: depending on your device and backup settings, Android may include App data in a device or cloud backup. Backup copies are controlled by your operating-system and backup provider settings and may remain after the App is uninstalled.
  • Google advertising data is retained according to Google's policies and your Google account and advertising settings.
  • Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain a record of the issue, protect the App, or meet legal obligations.

Controls available to you

7. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Change notification and haptic preferences in Fasting Roulette's Settings screen.
  • Revoke notifications or exact-alarm access in Android system settings.
  • When Google requires a privacy-options entry for your region, open Settings > Privacy > Privacy choices in the App to review or change your advertising consent.
  • Use Google My Ad Center and your Android advertising controls to manage ad personalization and your advertising ID.
  • Delete local App data by clearing Fasting Roulette's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App. Manage any device backups separately through your Android or Google backup settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. For information held directly by us, such as a support email, contact us using the address below. For information controlled by Google, use Google's privacy and account controls or contact Google.

Protecting information

8. Security

Keeping core records on your device limits their exposure to us. Access to those records depends on the security of your device, Android backups, and any accounts connected to those backups. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Age limitations

9. Children

Fasting Roulette is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 or under the applicable age of digital consent in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

Fasting can carry health risks and may not be appropriate for minors. The App provides a timer, game, and general educational descriptions; it does not provide medical advice or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
